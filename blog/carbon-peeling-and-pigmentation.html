Carbon peeling and uneven skin tone: prevention and professional care

+36 1 707 0075

Uneven skin tone and dark spots are among the most common reasons clients visit a skin clinic. Prevention and professional care work best together. This article outlines the main causes of pigmentation, the everyday habits that help, and the role of carbon peeling.

Why does pigmentation appear?

Pigmentation results from an increased or uneven production of melanin, the pigment that gives skin its colour. Common triggers include:

  • ultraviolet exposure from the sun
  • hormonal changes
  • marks left after acne or skin irritation
  • natural skin ageing

Any new, changing or irregular spot should be examined by a dermatologist before any cosmetic treatment.

Prevention starts with sun protection

Daily broad-spectrum sunscreen is the single most important habit for preventing new pigmentation, including on cloudy days and in winter. Reapply during long periods outdoors, and wear a hat and sunglasses in strong sun.

What is carbon peeling?

Carbon peeling is a non-invasive laser facial. A thin layer of carbon lotion is applied to the skin, and a laser then passes over the treated area. The treatment is commonly used to deep-cleanse pores, refine the look of skin texture and support a brighter, more even complexion. It is often described as comfortable, and there is generally no downtime.

Is carbon peeling suitable for me?

Suitability depends on your skin type, current skincare and medical history. Your therapist will assess your skin at the consultation. Recent sun exposure, certain medications or active skin conditions may mean postponing the treatment.

Aftercare

  • use a gentle cleanser and moisturiser for the following days
  • apply sunscreen every morning
  • avoid strong exfoliants unless your therapist advises otherwise

Frequently asked questions

How many sessions are recommended?

A course of several sessions is often suggested; the number depends on your skin goals and is discussed individually.

Can I wear make-up afterwards?

Your therapist will advise you; many clients prefer to let the skin rest for the remainder of the day.

Does carbon peeling remove pigmentation permanently?

No treatment can guarantee permanent removal. Consistent sun protection is essential to maintain any improvement.
