Collagen and skin quality: how exosome therapy and mesotherapy fit in

+36 1 707 0075

Collagen is a structural protein that gives the skin its firmness and elasticity. Understanding how it changes over time helps in choosing sensible skincare and clinic treatments.

Why does collagen decline?

The body's own collagen production gradually slows with age. Several external factors can accelerate this process:

  • unprotected sun exposure
  • smoking
  • insufficient sleep and chronic stress
  • a diet low in protein and fresh produce

Everyday habits that protect your skin

  • daily sunscreen
  • a consistent, gentle skincare routine
  • balanced nutrition and adequate sleep

Mesotherapy with microneedles

Microneedle mesotherapy creates very fine, controlled micro-channels in the skin's surface. This process is intended to support the skin's natural renewal and allows carefully selected active ingredients to be applied. Mild redness for a short period afterwards is common. Your therapist will explain what to expect and how to care for your skin.

Exosome therapy

Exosome therapy is a newer approach in aesthetic skincare. Exosome-based products are applied to the skin, often in combination with microneedling, with the aim of supporting skin quality, radiance and texture. As research in this field is still developing, we discuss the treatment, its aims and its limitations openly with every client during the consultation.

Which option is right for you?

The choice depends on your skin condition, goals and medical history. Some clients benefit from one method; others from a combined plan. Pregnancy, active skin infections and certain medical conditions may be contraindications.

Frequently asked questions

When can I see a change?

The skin renews gradually, so changes develop over the weeks following treatment and vary individually.

Is there downtime?

Temporary redness or sensitivity may occur; most clients resume normal activities quickly. Your therapist will give personal aftercare instructions.

Can collagen supplements replace treatments?

Supplements are not a substitute for professional treatment or sun protection. Please consult your doctor before taking supplements.
