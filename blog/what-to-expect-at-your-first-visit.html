What to expect at your first visit to Infinite Care

+36 1 707 0075

Visiting a beauty clinic for the first time can feel like a big step. Here is a simple overview of how a first visit to Infinite Care Beauty Clinic usually goes, so you know what to expect.

Before you arrive

The clinic is in the 6th district of Budapest, at Podmaniczky street 5. We are open Monday to Friday from 9 to 20 and on Saturdays from 9 to 15. You can book by phone, so please call us or send an email to choose a time that suits you.

  • Arrive a few minutes early so you can settle in without rushing.
  • Come with clean skin if you are booked for a facial or another skin treatment.
  • Bring a list of any medications, allergies or skin conditions you would like us to know about.

The consultation

Every visit starts with a conversation. Your specialist will ask about your goals, your skin or health background and any questions you have. Together you will talk through the options, so you can decide what feels right for you. Nobody will pressure you to choose anything.

Your treatment

Treatments take place in a calm, private treatment room. Your specialist will explain each step before it begins and will adjust things to keep you comfortable. If you feel unsure at any point, simply say so.

After your visit

You will receive aftercare advice that fits the treatment you chose. If you have questions once you are home, you are welcome to contact us. Some treatments are planned as a series, and your specialist can talk you through a schedule that suits you.

We look forward to welcoming you.

General information only, not medical advice. Your specialist will advise you individually at the clinic.
